Board of Commissioners minutes, September 29, 2026

Elmstead County Board of Commissioners, regular session, September 29, 2026.

Resolution 2026-118: On a motion by Commissioner Hale, seconded by Commissioner Ortiz, the Board voted 3-0 to adopt a twelve-month moratorium on the acceptance of applications for data centers in the unincorporated areas of Elmstead County, effective immediately.

The moratorium expires September 29, 2027 unless the Board ends it sooner.
