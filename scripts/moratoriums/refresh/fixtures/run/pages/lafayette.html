Lafayette council approves data center moratorium

LAFAYETTE, Ind. - The Lafayette City Council voted 6-1 on October 5, 2026 to adopt a one-year moratorium on new data center applications within city limits.

The pause covers rezonings, site plans and building permits for data centers while the plan commission drafts standards for water use, noise and power.

Lafayette is in Tippecanoe County. Council members said the moratorium can end early if the new ordinance is adopted.
