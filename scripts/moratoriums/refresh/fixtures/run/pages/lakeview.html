Lakeview bans data centers

LAKEVIEW, N.J. - The Borough Council on Wednesday, September 16, 2026 adopted Ordinance 2026-14, which prohibits data centers in every zoning district in the borough.

The ordinance adds a definition of data center to the land use code. Lakeview is in Halsey County.
