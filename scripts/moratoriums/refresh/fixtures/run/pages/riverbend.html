Riverbend Electric Cooperative pauses large-load requests

On September 24, 2026 the board of Riverbend Electric Cooperative voted to pause the acceptance of new large-load service requests of 10 MW or more, including data centers and cryptocurrency mining, until its wholesale supplier completes a transmission study.

The cooperative serves members in Ardmore and Bell counties in Kansas.
