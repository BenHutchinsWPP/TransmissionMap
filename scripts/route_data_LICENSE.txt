TransmissionMap line-routing data
=================================

This directory holds the data the TransmissionMap route planner reads: the
250 m route cube (250m/), crossing-report layers (report/), the Routing
constraints map tiles (constraints/) and aviation screening files
(report/screening/). It combines sources under several licences.
Each file family carries the licence of its sources, as listed below.
Per-band sources, vintages and processing rules are recorded in
250m/grid.json and in report/screening/manifest.json.

3. FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer - acknowledgement
----------------------------------------------------
Flood hazard areas are derived from the FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer
(NFHL), Key Layers edition 2026-10 (NFHL_Key_Layers_202610). The NFHL is a
US federal work; FEMA asks that it be acknowledged as the source.
Source: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer.
In: 250m/pd_*.tif band 19 (nfhl), constraints/fema-flood, fema-flood-map,
what-constrains. This is a screening derivative at 250 m, not an official
flood map or a flood-zone determination, and is not endorsed by FEMA.
